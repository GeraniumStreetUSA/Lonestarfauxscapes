# Image prompts: Lone Star rebuild

Owner rule: Claude never generates images. These are prompts for you to shoot or commission. **Real installs always win**: if you have a real photo for a slot, use it and skip the prompt.

**Slot key**
- **REAL ONLY**: must be a real Lone Star or Geranium Street install photo. Location captions only if you confirm the place.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE OK**: owner-made illustrative imagery is acceptable if it is never captioned as a project or a place.

**Every prompt excludes:** people's faces, brand logos, signage text, Texas flags or stars, cowhide, oversaturated greens, plastic sheen, visible seams that look unfinished, watermarks, and CGI or render look.

## Slots that still need images

### 1. Hero, larger original (REAL ONLY)
Ask first for the original of the Austin job at 2400 px or wider. If you reshoot a new job:
- **Scene:** a residential backyard privacy wall of boxwood panels on a tall fence, filling 60% of the frame, a live oak trunk and canopy in front casting crisp shadow on the green.
- **Light:** hard midday Texas sun with high contrast, or low gold evening light from camera left.
- **Lens and crop:** 35 mm, eye level, landscape. Leave negative space on the left third for the headline.
- **Size:** 2400 x 1600 (3:2), plus a 1200 x 1500 (4:5) phone crop centred on the wall and oak.

### 2. Realism close-up, "Will it look fake?" (REAL ONLY)
- **Scene:** a macro of the installed boxwood hedge face showing leaf density and one panel seam where two 20 in panels meet. Light raking across the leaves to show depth.
- **Light:** side light, late afternoon.
- **Lens and crop:** 100 mm macro, straight on, seam running vertically through the right third.
- **Size:** 1600 x 1600 (1:1).

### 3. System card: artificial hedge (REAL preferred, ILLUSTRATIVE OK)
- **Scene:** boxwood hedge panels raising a cedar board-on-board fence, DFW suburban backyard, a neighbor's two-story house partly hidden behind.
- **Light:** midday, crisp shadow.
- **Lens and crop:** 28 mm, slight low angle.
- **Size:** 1600 x 1200 (4:3).

### 4. System card: living wall (REAL preferred, ILLUSTRATIVE OK)
- **Scene:** a mixed-foliage artificial living wall on a limestone or stucco courtyard wall, San Antonio or Hill Country home, terracotta pots and a steel bench in front.
- **Light:** open shade with warm bounce.
- **Lens and crop:** 35 mm, straight on.
- **Size:** 1600 x 1200 (4:3).

### 5. System card: fence extension (REAL preferred, ILLUSTRATIVE OK)
- **Scene:** hedge panels mounted on a steel frame above an existing 6 ft wood fence, adding about 2 ft of height. Pool deck edge in the foreground, concrete coping visible.
- **Light:** midday.
- **Lens and crop:** 24 mm from the pool deck.
- **Size:** 1600 x 1200 (4:3).

### 6. System card: commercial wall / Vallum FRX (REAL ONLY)
- **Scene:** a fire-rated artificial wall on a restaurant patio or storefront facade, Houston or Dallas, with tables in the foreground and no patrons' faces.
- **Light:** golden hour or even overcast.
- **Lens and crop:** 35 mm.
- **Size:** 1600 x 1200 (4:3).

### 7. Before / after pair (REAL ONLY)
- **Scene:** the same tripod position before and after an install, along a fence line, with the same light and time of day. This unlocks a future before/after section.
- **Size:** 2000 x 1333 each (3:2).

### 8. Process step photos (REAL ONLY)
Four photos at the same aspect: site measuring with a tape on a fence, panels staged on a truck or in the shop, a crew fixing panels, and the finished run. Faces turned away or out of frame.
- **Size:** 1200 x 900 (4:3).

### 9. Metro hub banners, x5: Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Austin, San Antonio (REAL ONLY when captioned)
- A real install in that metro, or no image (a plan drawing is used instead).
- **Size:** 1600 x 900 (16:9).

### 10. Open Graph / social share image (ILLUSTRATIVE OK)
- **Composition:** the hero photo cropped to 1200 x 630, with the wordmark only (added in design, not in the photo).
- **Size:** 1200 x 630.

### 11. Guide headers: heat and UV, HOA, fire code, maintenance, cost (ILLUSTRATIVE OK)
- **Default:** a site-plan drawing (no photo needed). Photo prompts can be added on request.

### 12. Freestanding privacy wall unit (REAL ONLY, needed for `/privacy-wall`)
- **Scene:** one or two 4 ft x 4 ft freestanding artificial hedge units on their standard bases, set on a Texas patio or pool deck, screening a seating area. Late-afternoon side light, eye-level, 35 mm.
- **Must show:** the base and the panel edges clearly, so the unit reads as freestanding (not fence-mounted).
- **Must not show:** faces, house numbers, other brands.
- **Size:** 1200 x 900 (4:3). Today the page uses an uncaptioned fence-line hedge photo as a stand-in.

### 13. Living wall at a pool (REAL ONLY, needed for `/pool-living-wall`)
- **Scene:** an artificial living wall on a pool-side wall or cabana in Texas, with the water in the foreground. Bright midday or golden hour, 24 to 35 mm, level horizon.
- **Must not show:** people in swimwear, faces, house numbers.
- **Size:** 1200 x 900 (4:3). Today the page uses a foliage close-up as a stand-in.
